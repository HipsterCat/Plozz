# Plozz Home

Home brings together the content available to a profile without changing access
to its underlying media sources.

## Language

**Home section**:
A configurable part of Home, such as Continue Watching, a library's Recently
Added row, Live TV, or the Hero.
_Avoid_: Widget, module

**Row**:
A horizontal collection of cards within a Home section.

**Hero**:
The optional featured area anchored at the top of Home. Its content and appearance
are configurable, but its position is not.

**Showcase**:
The Apple TV layout whose preview follows the focused card. The preview belongs
to the page layout rather than to a movable section.

**Home layout preset**:
A starting arrangement of Home sections. Match server follows available server
arrangements; Combined rows starts with cross-library rows.

**Channel shortcut**:
A Home card that opens a live channel. It is not a resumable movie or episode.
